# Shift Trade Board Policy

## 1. Purpose
The Shift Trade Board supports safe, fair, and auditable shift coverage changes for Security Professionals while maintaining operational readiness and policy compliance.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all Security Professionals and Supervisors using the Shift Trade Board.

## 3. Policy Expectations
1. Officers may post only their own assigned shifts.
2. Only shifts in `Assigned` status are eligible to be posted.
3. Officers may request pickup only for `Posted` shifts and may not request their own shift.
4. Every trade request is subject to system validation checks:
- Role qualification
- Armed qualification (when required)
- Rest/overtime and overlap limits
5. Only one active pending request is allowed per posted shift at any time.
6. Supervisor review is required for final approval or denial.
7. Approved requests reassign the shift to the requesting officer and return the shift to `Assigned` status.
8. Denied requests are recorded and retained for audit.
9. All status changes and decisions must be auditable through system logs.
10. Notification messages must be generated for impacted officers after supervisor decision.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities
- Security Officer: Post eligible shifts, submit pickup requests, and monitor request outcomes.
- Supervisor: Review queue, confirm policy and staffing compliance, approve or deny requests.
- System: Enforce validation rules, status controls, notification creation, and audit capture.

## 5. Compliance
Failure to follow this policy may result in denied requests and supervisory follow-up. Exceptions must be approved by Security Leadership.
